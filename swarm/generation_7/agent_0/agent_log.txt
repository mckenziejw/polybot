## Results

### Hypothesis Validation
Both parts of the hypothesis are **supported** on the sample data:

1. **2s window beats 5s**: Win rate 52.9% vs 51.3%. Signal decays monotonically with longer windows — 30s window actually loses money (-4.64 PnL). Early snapshots clearly capture more informational content.

2. **Wider spreads amplify signal**: This is the strongest finding. The top spread quartile (Q4, avg spread 0.025) achieves **63.6% win rate and Sharpe 0.285**, while Q2/Q3 are below 50%. The interaction is dramatic — the signal is only useful in wide-spread markets.

### Caveats
- Only 59 trades on 917 markets — need validation on the full 7,187-market dataset
- The spread > 0.05 filter from the original hypothesis is too restrictive (only 6 markets), but the *direction* is correct: wider spreads help significantly
- Some risk of overfitting with the threshold/percentile sweep — the top config's 72.9% win rate may regress toward the ~63% seen in Q4 on out-of-sample data
