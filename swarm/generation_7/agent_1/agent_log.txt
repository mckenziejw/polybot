### Interpretation

1. **The original hypothesis (first 2h UTC) failed** — only 17 markets fell in that window after mid-price filtering, yielding just 6 trades with terrible performance. The sample dataset doesn't have enough concentration in early-UTC hours.

2. **The deep_level_ratio signal itself shows promise** — without the time filter, buying Down when DLR is low (deep ask > deep bid) worked well: 57.9% win rate on the Down side, driving most of the profit (+5.81 PnL).

3. **Asymmetry**: The signal is notably one-sided — buying Down on low DLR works much better than buying Up on high DLR. This suggests deep ask-side liquidity concentration in early snapshots is a meaningful signal for Down outcomes.

4. **Caveat**: This is a 401-market sample (only markets with resolution data matched). Needs validation on the full 7,187-market dataset before any confidence in the signal.
