### Key findings:

1. **The signal IS predictive at the market level** — quintile analysis shows markets with high mean deep ratio resolve Down 81% of the time vs 31% for low. This is a very strong effect.

2. **But it requires look-ahead** — the full-window mean (using all ~60s of snapshots) gives 63.7% WR / 4.33 Sharpe, but this uses future information. The expanding mean (no look-ahead) drops to 52.2% WR / 0.53 Sharpe.

3. **The signal doesn't stabilize quickly enough** — at 5s of observation, the expanding mean has only 0.03 correlation with the full-window mean. The deep ratio is very noisy snapshot-to-snapshot.

4. **The original hypothesis threshold (0.3) was far too low** — 95.7% of snapshots exceed it. The median deep ratio is 2.65.

5. **High-threshold variant is interesting but low-N** — threshold=10.0 gives 57-65% WR but only 23-75 trades, insufficient for statistical significance.

The Down-side asymmetry exists but isn't exploitable in real-time with only early orderbook data. The signal needs more observation time than what's available before entry.
