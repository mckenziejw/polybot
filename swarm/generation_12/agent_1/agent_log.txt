The original hypothesis is **structurally invalid** on Polymarket. Up and Down tokens share mirrored order books (`bid_Up + ask_Down = 1.0`), so `spread_Up ≡ spread_Down` at all times. The spread divergence signal is identically zero — it can never fire.
