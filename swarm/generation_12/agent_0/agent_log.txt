## Strategy: Entropy-Weighted Imbalance — Verdict: **Marginal**

### Key Finding
In Polymarket binary markets, Up and Down order books are **perfect mirrors** (Up bids = Down asks), so the original hypothesis of comparing combined entropy between sides was degenerate (ratio always = 1.0). After correcting to use **bid-side-only entropy**, genuine asymmetry exists (std of entropy diff = 0.36).

### Root Cause
In the first 5 seconds, market makers set symmetric books with similar depth profiles. Entropy differences are small and mostly reflect market-maker-specific quoting patterns, not informed directional positioning. The signal-to-noise ratio is too low for reliable prediction from static book snapshots alone.
